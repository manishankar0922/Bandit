## 1. Description of Service

Bandit is a client-side browser extension available for Mozilla Firefox and Chromium-based browsers. Its features include:
- Interactive prompt engineering, structure enhancement, and refinement.
- Curated prompt templates library with fill-in-the-blanks runners.
- Local webpage and conversation summarization.
- Gamified desktop pet interactions (animations, XP, accessories).
